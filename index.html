How to play
  • Goal: Clear all C capture targets, then land on G.
  • Move: Click a highlighted (legal) cell to move the Orb.
  • Modes: step = 4-direction. glide = 8-direction slide until blocked.
  • Editor: Turn Editor on, then Shift+Click to paint the selected tile.
  • Bumper (B): Forces the next move direction to reverse.
  • Hole (H): Landing on it returns you to the last safe tile.
